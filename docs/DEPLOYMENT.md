# Deployment Instructions

## Deploying to Vercel
1. Go to the [Vercel dashboard](https://vercel.com/dashboard) and sign in.
2. Click on the "New Project" button.
3. Import your GitHub repository.
4. Vercel will automatically detect the framework. Review the settings and adjust if necessary.
5. Click on the "Deploy" button.
6. Once deployed, you will receive a unique URL to access your application.

## Deploying to Railway
1. Visit the [Railway dashboard](https://railway.app) and sign in or create an account.
2. Click on the "New Project" button.
3. Choose the option to deploy from GitHub.
4. Select your repository and configure the deployment settings as needed.
5. Click on "Deploy" and wait for the process to finish.
6. After deployment, Railway will provide you with a URL to access your app.
